Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you manage properties in?

We specialise in Liverpool, Barrow-in-Furness, and Carlisle, with a focus on premium residential rentals in these key North West and Cumbria locations.

How long does the tenancy application process take?

Once we receive a complete application, we aim to provide a decision within 2–5 working days, depending on reference checks and landlord approval. You can track progress via our online portal.

Do you allow pets?

Many of our properties are pet-friendly, but it depends on the individual landlord. Please indicate your pet requirements in the application form and we will match you with suitable homes.

What is included in your management service?

Our full management package includes tenant finding & vetting, rent collection, maintenance coordination, regular inspections, compliance checks, and 24/7 emergency response — all handled with transparency and care.

How do I arrange a viewing?

Click "Arrange Viewing" on any property listing or contact us directly. We offer weekday, evening, and weekend viewings where possible to suit your schedule.

What deposit and fees should I expect?

Deposits are typically 5 weeks' rent. There are no hidden tenant fees under current UK regulations. All costs are clearly explained before you sign any agreement.
